InvoiceFlow is an open-source invoice generator built for Indian small businesses — GST-compliant line items and tax breakdowns out of the box, with Supabase (Postgres + auth) handling data and Documenso wired in for digital signatures on finished invoices.

**Stack**

- Next.js frontend and API routes
- Supabase for auth, storage, and the Postgres backend
- Documenso integration for e-signatures on generated invoices
- Deployed as a containerized app

**Why it's here**

Most invoicing tools either ignore GST specifics or lock the compliant version behind a subscription. InvoiceFlow is the version I wanted to exist: open, self-hostable, and compliant by default.
