A Monte Carlo simulation toolkit built for multi-brand forecast reporting — covering seven product lines, with @Risk driving the probabilistic modeling and VBA orchestrating the reporting layer.

**What it does**

- Runs Monte Carlo simulations across triangular, discrete, normal, and uniform distributions per input variable
- Exports results straight into PowerPoint, including backup slides per variable
- Renders tornado charts with correctly positioned labels across variable-input slides
