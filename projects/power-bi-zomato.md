A multi-page Power BI report built on Zomato restaurant data, with a home page navigation menu (via bookmarks) leading into separate Global and India reports.

**What it covers**

- Global report: restaurant counts by country, rating combo charts, city-level slicers
- India report: restaurant distribution by city, average rating and percent-unrated DAX measures
- Full DAX modeling and Power Query transformation from raw source data
