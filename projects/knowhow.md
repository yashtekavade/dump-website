KnowHow is a learning-platform scaffold for role-specific career roadmaps — the idea being that "how do I become an AI Engineer" deserves a guided path, not a scattered pile of blog posts and course links.

**Stack**

- React + Vite frontend
- Node/Express API layer
- Postgres (hosted on Neon) for roadmap and progress data

**Scope**

Initial tracks cover Data Engineering, GenAI, AI Engineering, and Data Analysis — each broken into a guided sequence of concepts and checkpoints rather than a flat resource list.
