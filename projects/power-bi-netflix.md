A multi-page Power BI report analyzing Netflix's content catalog, split into Global and India-focused analytics pages with KPI cards and DAX-driven metrics.

**What it covers**

- Global analytics: total content, movies vs. TV shows, top countries and genres
- India analytics: content added in India over time
- Area charts, donut charts, treemaps, and clustered bar charts with full bookmark/slicer navigation
