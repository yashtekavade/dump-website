This is the first entry in what's meant to be a running build-log — short notes on projects, tools, and things I'm learning, separate from the longer-form pieces on [Substack](https://yashtekavade.substack.com).

The site itself is the first thing worth writing about: it's a small static-site generator (Markdown + YAML frontmatter → Jinja templates → plain HTML), with each project and post treated like a pipeline "run" — a status, a set of tags, a timestamp. That framing isn't decorative; it's closer to how I actually think about the things I build, coming from a data engineering background.

More soon.
